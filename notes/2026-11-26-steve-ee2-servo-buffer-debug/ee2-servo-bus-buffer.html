EE2 servo bus buffer, as working

Date 2026-11-26 Board 74LS125AN half-duplex buffer, XIAO ESP32-C5 (EE2, 10:BD:A3:CD:D1:B8) Servo STS3215, ID 2 Based on drawing TC-EE2-BUF-001 rev C and the Discord diagram

Working. Servo ID 2 swung 30° each way from the Mac, through Board 10 over ESP-NOW to EE2, and every leg landed within 0.7° of its target.

What changed from the Discord version

The Discord diagram shows the transmitter, the receiver and the IC power. It does not show the 1.1 kΩ pull-up or name the ESP pins. As first built, the pull-up went from the servo DATA line to +5V and the TX wire sat on D9. Two wires moved on the board, and EE2 got new firmware.

ChangeAs first builtNowWhy
1.1 kΩ pull-upFrom servo DATA (U1 pin 4 side of the 220 Ω) to +5VFrom U1 pin 3 (chip side of the 220 Ω) to +5VThe pull-up current flowed through the 220 Ω, so a low on DATA only reached 1.04 V. The servo's spec sheet wants under 0.45 V. Now a low is 0.13 V.
TX wireXIAO D9XIAO D7 (GPIO12)The EE2 firmware sends on D7, as drawing TC-EE2-BUF-001 has it. On D9 its bytes never reached U1 pin 2.
EE2 firmwareHansi's buffered build (source not published)Build xiao_c5_ee2_buf: the published end-effector source with a buffered bus on D7/D6/D8, plus debug commands over the radioSo the build can be reproduced from source, and so any servo ID can be driven by hand through Board 10.

Unchanged: R1 and R2 330 Ω, R3 10 kΩ, R4 220 Ω, R5 2.2 kΩ, D1 1N5819, C1 100 nF, RX on D6, DIR on D8.

The three changes were not tested one at a time. The servo first answered after all the wiring changes were in and EE2 was back on board power, so this page cannot say which of them was strictly needed. The 1.04 V low was outside the servo's spec either way.

Power rules from the team notes still hold: the XIAO runs from USB or from the buck on its 5V pad, never both. The servo's GND and the pack's − must be joined to the XIAO's GND, whichever way the XIAO is powered. On this board that link comes through the buck, so it is missing when the XIAO runs from the Mac's USB with the buck disconnected.

Debug commands ride inside the existing end-effector command frame and replies come back inside the servo report frame, so Board 10 needed no reflash. Debug moves ignore the dock travel limits but keep a torque cap (5 to 60 %), a speed cap, the load and stall stop, and a 10-second torque-off after the last command.

Open items

  • On the Mac's USB there is no shared ground. With EE2 powered from the Mac, the loop test passes and the echo is clean, but servo ID 2 stays silent. Steve confirmed the cause: the pack's − is not joined to the XIAO's GND in that setup. On board power the buck provides the link and the servo answers. To use USB, add a wire from pack − (or the servo's GND pin) to the XIAO's GND.
  • Reply rate. With Hansi's build on board power, servo 2 answered about two in three telemetry reads (404 of 602). The rate with the new build was not measured.
  • Spare gates. Drawing TC-EE2-BUF-001 ties U1 pins 9 and 12 to GND and pins 10 and 13 to +5V. Pin 13 read 1.6 V on this board earlier in the day, which suggests it is not connected. Tie the spare enables to +5V as drawn.
  • The drawing has the same pull-up position. Rev C puts R6 (1 kΩ) from DATA to +5V. By the same arithmetic that gives a low of about 1.1 V, so the drawing should move R6 to U1 pin 3 too.
  • The header is labelled for servo ID 2. Nothing answers at ID 1 on this board.
  • Hansi's buffered source is still unpublished. The new build replaces it on EE2; the two were not compared line by line.
