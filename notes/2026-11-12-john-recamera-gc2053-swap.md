# GC2053 board swap unlocks M12 lenses, drops LEDs

Written by John's AI agent from web research on 2026-11-12. Not authoritative.

This follows the "reCamera 2002w fisheye" section of the [11-06 claw camera note](2026-11-06-john-claw-camera-followups.md). It is analysis only and changes nothing in `truth/`. Tags: **[Seeed]** for Seeed pages and PDFs, **[schematics]** and **[source code]** for files read in Seeed's and Sophgo's repos, **[one user report]**, **[team file]** for our own 11-05 test files, and **[calculated]** or **[inferred]** for this note's own work. Nothing here is [verified].

## Bottom line

**Can we do it? Yes, on paper.** Seeed's store page says you can buy a finished 2002 (W) and "install GC2053 Sensor Board onto the finished reCamera unit" ([Seeed store](https://www.seeedstudio.com/reCamera-2002-Sensor-Board-GC2053-p-6556.html)). Its fisheye page says a 2002w, including our SKU 102991897, needs this board (SKU 100041077) for any lens change ([Seeed fisheye](https://www.seeedstudio.com/J0018027B-p-6647.html)) [Seeed]. The S401 (GC2053) and the stock S101 (OV5647) share the same outline, 60-pin core connector, pin map and mounting holes [schematics]. reCamera-OS 0.2.1 and later detects the GC2053 at boot with no config change [source code]. One user has done the swap on a 2002W, and it streamed [one user report]. Seeed publishes no install procedure.

**What breaks.** The four white fill LEDs are gone [schematics]. The mic, speaker amp and status LEDs stay [schematics]. Peak resolution drops from 5 MP to 2 MP. Seeed tunes the image only for the stock 2.9 mm lens. The one user who fitted another lens got overexposure outdoors and a purple tint indoors, and Seeed sent tools, not a profile [one user report]. The camera gets roughly 8–9 mm taller, plus the lens [calculated, approximate]. Our on-camera tag code has no lens-distortion terms, so a fisheye needs a new calibration and a code change [team file].

**What to buy.** Per camera, one GC2053 board ($29.90) and one M12 fisheye (SKU 100095034, $9.90). Add one 2.5 mm "140°" wide lens (SKU 100076570, $7.90) to compare, because on paper it sees almost as much on this sensor. For the two cameras in [D-038](../truth/decisions.md) that is $87.50 at Seeed list prices before shipping [calculated]. The board's page shows no stock status today. We did not check Mouser.

**What to test first.**

1. Check which reCamera-OS version our 2002w runs. If it is 0.2.0 or older, update it (0.2.4 is the latest stable) and re-run the 11-05 tag test before touching hardware.
2. Fit the board on one unit. Confirm the web UI's device type ends in "(GC2053)" and that `i2cdetect -y -r 2` answers at 0x3f.
3. Check colour and exposure under lab lights with the stock S401 lens, then with each new lens.
4. Set focus. Measure sharpness at 20, 30, 50 and 100 cm, and measure the real horizontal field.
5. Calibrate with a fisheye model. Then measure on-camera detection rate and range at 640, 1280 and 1920 px wide, with tags at the centre and at the edge.
6. Measure the new camera height and give it to mech for the [D-039](../truth/decisions.md) mount.

## The S401 drops onto the same 60-pin connector

Both boards are 30.0 × 30.0 mm. Both put the core connector J1 (Sungtech ST-BTB-058060-B, 60 pins, 0.5 mm pitch) and both mounting holes at the same coordinates. The pin-to-net maps match pin for pin ([S401 PCB](https://github.com/Seeed-Studio/OSHW-reCamera-Series/blob/HEAD/hardware/recamera-2002/sensors/s401-gc2053/PCB/reCamera%202002%20S4%20GC2053%20Sensor%20Board_V1.0_SCH%26PCB_250905.kicad_pcb); [S101 PCB](https://github.com/Seeed-Studio/OSHW-reCamera-Series/blob/HEAD/hardware/recamera-2002/sensors/s101-ov5647/PCB/V1.1/reCamera%20S101%20v1.1.kicad_pcb)) [schematics]. Both route a MIPI clock plus 2 data lanes and the same I2C pair. On each board the image sensor sits on a separate camera module that plugs into a 24-pin Hirose DF30 connector.

**Swap whole boards, never camera modules.** The S401 feeds 3.3 V to all three module supply pins and relies on regulators inside the BaoXinFa module. The S101 makes 2.8 V and 1.5 V with its own LDOs ([S401 schematic](https://github.com/Seeed-Studio/OSHW-reCamera-Series/blob/HEAD/hardware/recamera-2002/sensors/s401-gc2053/PCB/reCamera%202002%20S4%20GC2053%20Sensor%20Board_V1.0_SCH%26PCB_250905.kicad_sch); [S101 schematic](https://github.com/Seeed-Studio/OSHW-reCamera-Series/blob/HEAD/hardware/recamera-2002/sensors/s101-ov5647/PCB/V1.1/reCamera%20S101%20v1.1.kicad_sch)). An OV5647 module on an S401 would see 3.3 V on rails rated to 3.0 V [inferred].

Seeed sells the board as SKU 100041077 for USD 29.90. The box holds the board, a plastic cover and four M2×10 screws ([Seeed store](https://www.seeedstudio.com/reCamera-2002-Sensor-Board-GC2053-p-6556.html)) [Seeed]. Seeed publishes no guide, video or step list for fitting it. This research checked the store page, the English and Chinese wikis and the hardware repo. The likely procedure is to remove the stock front cover and S101, press the S401 onto the same connector, and close it with the supplied cover and screws [inferred]. The stock cover will not do. Its largest opening is Φ12 mm, while the M12 holder is 13.2 mm square with a Φ14 barrel ([S101 cover STEP](https://github.com/Seeed-Studio/OSHW-reCamera-Series/blob/HEAD/hardware/recamera-2002/sensors/s101-ov5647/3D_Printed_Case/recamera-sensorcover-4040.stp); [module drawing](https://files.seeedstudio.com/wiki/reCamera/reCamera_hq_poe/lens_document.pdf)) [calculated from CAD].

The camera gets taller. Pakronics lists the S401 assembly at 40 × 40 × 26.4 mm ([Pakronics](https://www.pakronics.com.au/products/recamera-2002-sensor-board-gc2053-ss100041077)). The S101 cover is about 17–18 mm tall [calculated from the STEP file, approximate]. So expect roughly 8–9 mm more, plus lens protrusion. For scale, the 2002 series is 36.5 mm tall. The HQ PoE, which ships with this board, is 45.8 mm, or 53.1 mm with its lens ([2002 wiki](https://wiki.seeedstudio.com/recamera_2002_series_hardware_and_specs/); [HQ PoE wiki](https://wiki.seeedstudio.com/reCamera_hq_poe_hardware_and_specs/)) [Seeed]. There is no CAD for the S401 cover, so nobody knows yet whether the Φ20 mm fisheye front clears it. The public S401 files are V1.0, and the repo says to confirm the board revision before service work ([S401 README](https://github.com/Seeed-Studio/OSHW-reCamera-Series/blob/main/hardware/recamera-2002/sensors/s401-gc2053/README.md)).

The HQ PoE already has this board and an M12 mount. It is not a shortcut for us. Its Wi-Fi module is removed ([HQ PoE wiki](https://wiki.seeedstudio.com/reCamera_hq_poe_hardware_and_specs/)), and D-038 notes the PoE version was out of stock.

## reCamera-OS 0.2.1 and later finds the sensor by itself

Seeed states no minimum OS version anywhere. The code does. The GC2053 driver entered the eMMC build on 2025-08-05 ([commit 5076e4b](https://github.com/Seeed-Studio/reCamera-OS/commit/5076e4b17254037813d3849dfbb81e258a877870)). It is absent at tag 0.2.0 and present at tag 0.2.1 ([0.2.0 defconfig](https://github.com/Seeed-Studio/reCamera-OS/blob/0.2.0/external/build/boards/cv181x/sg2002_recamera_emmc/sg2002_recamera_emmc_defconfig); [0.2.1 defconfig](https://github.com/Seeed-Studio/reCamera-OS/blob/0.2.1/external/build/boards/cv181x/sg2002_recamera_emmc/sg2002_recamera_emmc_defconfig)). The camera app gained GC2053 in the same release ([sscma 0.2.0](https://github.com/Seeed-Studio/sscma-example-sg200x/blob/0.2.0/components/sophgo/video/src/video_paramparse.c); [sscma 0.2.1](https://github.com/Seeed-Studio/sscma-example-sg200x/blob/0.2.1/components/sophgo/video/src/video_paramparse.c)) [source code]. No release note mentions it ([releases](https://github.com/Seeed-Studio/reCamera-OS/releases)). The latest stable release is 0.2.4 (2026-03-23). 0.2.5 and 0.2.6 are pre-releases.

One eMMC image serves every 2002 variant, the HQ PoE included ([board dirs](https://github.com/Seeed-Studio/reCamera-OS/tree/0529ca0a8bc6b6b88a70c8b15e40d77c9e01cb82/external/build/boards/cv181x)). At boot the camera app probes I2C bus 2 for OV5647 (0x36), then SC530AI (0x30), then GC2053 (0x3f). It loads the matching ISP tuning file ([video_paramparse.c](https://github.com/Seeed-Studio/sscma-example-sg200x/blob/4926df702acba990b2ebd36a086f171a7964466e/components/sophgo/video/src/video_paramparse.c)). The web UI then appends "(OV5647)" or "(GC2053)" to the device type ([main.sh](https://github.com/Seeed-Studio/sscma-example-sg200x/blob/4926df702acba990b2ebd36a086f171a7964466e/solutions/supervisor/rootfs/usr/share/supervisor/scripts/main.sh); [api_device.cpp](https://github.com/Seeed-Studio/sscma-example-sg200x/blob/4926df702acba990b2ebd36a086f171a7964466e/solutions/supervisor/main/src/api_device.cpp)) [source code]. A Seeed maintainer confirmed the auto-detection in January 2026 ([sscma #36](https://github.com/Seeed-Studio/sscma-example-sg200x/issues/36)). Both sensors use the same bus, lane map and power-enable GPIO, so no device-tree change is needed ([cvi_board_init.c](https://github.com/Seeed-Studio/reCamera-OS/blob/0529ca0a8bc6b6b88a70c8b15e40d77c9e01cb82/external/build/boards/cv181x/sg2002_recamera_emmc/u-boot/cvi_board_init.c); [S70hardware](https://github.com/Seeed-Studio/reCamera-OS/blob/0529ca0a8bc6b6b88a70c8b15e40d77c9e01cb82/external/buildroot/board/cvitek/CV181X/overlay/etc/init.d/S70hardware)).

Our own pipeline reads the camera's RTSP stream through ffmpeg ([start_tags.sh](2026-11-05-john-recamera-test/on-camera/start_tags.sh)). It should keep running once the stream is up [inferred]. Its hard-coded 640 × 480 size and focal length will not carry over. The GC2053 is 16:9 native, so check what a 640 × 480 stream does with it (crop or scale).

**Detection is solved. Image quality is ours.** Seeed ships one GC2053 tuning file. It has a calibrated lens-shading table, and distortion correction is switched off ([gcore_gc2053_sdr.json](https://github.com/Seeed-Studio/reCamera-OS/blob/0529ca0a8bc6b6b88a70c8b15e40d77c9e01cb82/external/isp_tuning/cv181x/src/gcore_gc2053/gcore_gc2053_sdr.json)) [source code]. That suits the stock lens, which Seeed lists at 0.5% distortion. With a fisheye, expect wrong brightness and colour in the corners until shading is recalibrated [inferred]. The SG2002 SDK has lens-distortion-correction APIs. Neither the camera app nor the Node-RED camera node exposes them, and there is no fisheye-dewarp API ([cvi_gdc.h](https://github.com/sophgo/cvi_mpi/blob/6dbbbb8bed877cd3a08f76ce4aed61645c38bbe1/include/cvi_gdc.h); [cvi_vi_ctx.h](https://github.com/sophgo/cvi_mpi/blob/6dbbbb8bed877cd3a08f76ce4aed61645c38bbe1/include/linux/cvi_vi_ctx.h); [camera.cpp](https://github.com/Seeed-Studio/sscma-example-sg200x/blob/4926df702acba990b2ebd36a086f171a7964466e/solutions/sscma-node/main/node/camera.cpp)). An IR tuning file ships, but nothing loads it ([isp.c](https://github.com/Seeed-Studio/sscma-example-sg200x/blob/4926df702acba990b2ebd36a086f171a7964466e/components/sophgo/video/src/isp.c)). Neither board has IR-cut hardware [schematics]. Whether the stock S401 lens holder has IR-cut glass is not published. Seeed's answer for tuning is its CviPQTool plus Sophgo's ISP guides ([release 0.0.1 assets](https://github.com/Seeed-Studio/reCamera-OS/releases/tag/0.0.1); [Sophgo ISP Tuning Guide](https://doc.sophgo.com/cvitek-develop-docs/master/docs_latest_release/CV180x_CV181x/en/01.software/ISP/ISP_Tuning_Guide/build/html/index.html)).

## One user did this swap, and tuning was the problem

The only first-hand public report is GitHub user vikidev01, in February 2026 ([OSHW #89](https://github.com/Seeed-Studio/OSHW-reCamera-Series/issues/89)). They fitted the GC2053 board to a 2002W and changed to an unnamed lens. They said the board worked and fit the connector. Outdoors the image was heavily overexposed, and auto-exposure adapted poorly. Indoors it had a steady purple/magenta tint, which they blamed on a missing IR-cut filter. Seeed replied 13 days later with Sophgo's ISP docs, CviPQTool and a GC2053 `sensor_cfg.ini`. The user got the tuning tool connected but no good result. A Seeed engineer then said he lacked the same lens and could not reproduce it. The IR-cut question was never answered, and the issue was auto-closed as stale. A July follow-up asking for an official 2002W + GC2053 tuning profile got no reply ([OSHW #94](https://github.com/Seeed-Studio/OSHW-reCamera-Series/issues/94)). A June question about lights on the GC2053 board also went unanswered ([OSHW #93](https://github.com/Seeed-Studio/OSHW-reCamera-Series/issues/93)).

This is one user with a non-stock lens. It says nothing yet about the stock S401 lens. It does show the support pattern. Replies take one to two weeks, deep questions get generic docs, and a bot closes issues after 21 days.

No public report of AprilTag or ArUco detection on any reCamera turned up. Our 11-05 test may be the only one. Users report that custom OpenCV pipelines on the SG2002 are slow and only work below 1080p ([sscma #21](https://github.com/Seeed-Studio/sscma-example-sg200x/issues/21)), and that rotating frames in OpenCV is CPU-heavy ([reCamera-OS #44](https://github.com/Seeed-Studio/reCamera-OS/issues/44)). No independent review of GC2053 image quality on a reCamera exists. A Hackster test in total darkness covered only the OV5647 2002, which showed a black frame ([Hackster](https://www.hackster.io/xinrui666vvv/the-0-lux-test-five-ai-cameras-walk-into-a-dark-room-27e9d5)). Seeed's low-light claim for the GC2053 is unverified.

## The fisheye fills the sensor but likely sees about 135°, not 160°

The M12 holder sits on the BaoXinFa BXF4677 camera module, not on the S401 PCB. The stock lens is 2.9 mm, 93° D / 84° H / 54° V. It is focused at the factory for 39–69.5 cm and is not glued, so it can be refocused ([module drawing](https://files.seeedstudio.com/wiki/reCamera/reCamera_hq_poe/lens_document.pdf); [HQ PoE wiki](https://wiki.seeedstudio.com/reCamera_hq_poe_hardware_and_specs/)) [Seeed].

| Seeed lens | SKU | Price | Seeed's numbers |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Fisheye 1.8 mm](https://www.seeedstudio.com/J0018027B-p-6647.html) | 100095034 | $9.90 | 160° H, F1.8, MOD 0.2 m, manual focus, Φ20 × 18.5 mm, 7 g, image circle Φ6.7 mm, 53% distortion |
| [Wide 2.5 mm](https://www.seeedstudio.com/JY-127A025FB-3MP-p-6644.html) | 100076570 | $7.90 | "140°" in the name, 131° H for the GC2053 per its sheet, F2.2, MOD 0.2 m, Φ14 × 15.5 mm |
| Telephoto ([HQ PoE add-on list](https://www.seeedstudio.com/reCamera-2002-HQ-PoE-64GB-p-6557.html)) | not checked | $11.90 | 20°, 5 MP |
| [Ultra telephoto 22 mm](https://www.seeedstudio.com/22mm-B-p-6646.html) | 100070335 | $13.90 | 15° H, 2 MP |

The fisheye and ultra-telephoto pages say the 2002/2002W needs board 100041077, and that the Gimbal cannot change lenses. The wide page makes no compatibility statement [Seeed].

The fisheye's image circle (Φ6.7 mm) is larger than the GC2053's 6.17 mm active diagonal (1920 × 1080 at 2.8 µm). So the whole frame is lit, with no black ring [calculated]. The advertised 160° H matches the spec sheet's 1/2.5" row (161° H). The same sheet gives 143° H on a 1/2.7" sensor, which is larger than the GC2053, and it has no 1/2.9" row ([fisheye sheet](https://files.seeedstudio.com/wiki/reCamera/Various%20materials/Fisheye%20Technical%20Specification%20Sheet%281%29.pdf)). Scaling that table to the GC2053 gives about 135° H / 76° V [calculated, model-dependent]. Measure it before relying on it.

On paper the cheaper wide lens sees almost as much. Its sheet gives 131° H and says the figure is based on the GC2053 active area, though the row is labelled 1/2.7" ([wide sheet](https://files.seeedstudio.com/wiki/reCamera/Various%20materials/Wide%20Angle%20Technical%20Specification%20Sheet.pdf)). Its 2.5 mm focal length puts about 40% more pixels on a centred tag than the 1.8 mm fisheye [calculated]. There are two catches. Its sheet says fixed focus, while Seeed's product PDF says manual focus ([wide PDF](https://files.seeedstudio.com/Bazaar/product_pdf/100076570.pdf)). And the sheet calls it an IR-sensitive lens, which fits the purple-tint risk in #89 [inferred]. Buy both and compare. Whether a 131° lens meets [D-037](../truth/decisions.md)'s "must be fisheye" is the team's call.

Third-party M12 fisheyes exist. Most 180° ones found are specified for larger sensors, such as 1/2.3" ([Arducam](https://www.arducam.com/product/arducam-180-degree-fisheye-1-2-3-m12-mount-with-lens-adapter-for-raspberry-pi-high-quality-camera/); [Commonlands CIL292](https://commonlands.com/products/180-ftheta-m12-fisheye-lens-cil292); [Hampo GC2053 module](https://www.hampotech.com/2mp-gc2053-micro-fixed-focus-mipi-camera-module-product/)). None was checked for fit in the BXF4677 holder.

## For docking, the swap trades pixels for field

**The stock camera is narrower than we thought.** Our own calibration of the stock 2002w at 1920 × 1080 measured 44.0° H × 25.6° V, from one ChArUco run with 14 views and 0.30 px RMS ([calibration-1080p.json](2026-11-05-john-recamera-test/calibration-1080p.json)) [team file]. That matches a centre crop of the OV5647's native pixels (42.5° H), not its full sensor (55° H) [calculated]. The "65°" figure is the full-sensor diagonal. The GC2053's 1080p is its whole sensor. So even the stock S401 lens roughly doubles the horizontal field, to 84° H [Seeed].

Pixels across a 5 cm tag at the image centre, using a pinhole model at the nominal focal length. Edge tags get fewer, especially with the fisheye [calculated]:

| Setup | Horizontal field | 1920 wide: 20 / 50 / 100 cm | 640 wide: 20 / 50 / 100 cm |
|---|---|---|---|
| Stock 2002w, 1080p stream | 44° (team calibration) | 593 / 237 / 119 | 198 / 79 / 40 |
| GC2053 + stock 2.9 mm | 84° (Seeed) | 259 / 104 / 52 | 86 / 35 / 17 |
| GC2053 + 2.5 mm wide | 131° (Seeed sheet) | 223 / 89 / 45 | 74 / 30 / 15 |
| GC2053 + 1.8 mm fisheye | 160° advertised, ~135° calculated | 161 / 64 / 32 | 54 / 21 / 11 |

What counts as enough? Our 11-05 log gives a rough answer ([tag_log.csv](2026-11-05-john-recamera-test/tag_log.csv)) [team file]. It ran OpenCV's detector on the Mac, on downscaled 1080p frames, during one handheld run. Tags under 20 px across were rarely found. Tags of 20–30 px were found anywhere from about a quarter to nearly all of the time. Tags of 40 px or more were found nearly every time.

By that yardstick, the fisheye at 640 px wide keeps a 5 cm tag at 40 px only within about 27 cm. It drops under 20 px beyond about 54 cm. At 1920 px wide it holds 40 px out to about 80 cm [calculated]. The 3 cm tag that read at about 70 px in the 11-05 test would read at about 26 px with the fisheye, at the same width and distance [calculated]. So the fisheye needs detection at 1280 or 1920 px wide, or bigger tags. On-camera speed at those sizes is unmeasured. The 11-06 note measured mostly 100–150 ms per detection at 640 × 480. Our `tagstream` also sets `quad_decimate` to 2 ([tagstream.c](2026-11-05-john-recamera-test/on-camera/tagstream.c)), which finds quads at half that resolution [inferred].

**Distortion needs code.** The fisheye's 53% distortion needs a fisheye camera model, such as OpenCV's fisheye module. A pinhole calibration will not do [inferred]. `tagstream` passes only fx, fy, cx and cy to `estimate_tag_pose`, and [calibrate_screen.py](2026-11-05-john-recamera-test/calibrate_screen.py) fits a pinhole model with k1–k3 [team file]. We do not need dewarped video. Undistorting the four corners of each tag before pose is enough [inferred]. Tags near the edge will look curved, so test detection there.

**Focus favours the fisheye.** Focused at 0.3 m, the fisheye is sharp from about 16 cm to 3.9 m. The stock S401 lens covers about 28 cm to 2.1 m at its factory focus, or about 20–51 cm if refocused to 29 cm. The stock OV5647, focused for a 20 cm near limit, covers only about 20–27 cm [calculated, circle of confusion 2 px]. Only the fisheye covers 20 cm to 1 m with one setting.

**Light favours the GC2053.** Its 2.8 µm pixels and faster lenses collect about 4–10× more light per pixel than the OV5647 at F2.8 [calculated]. That allows shorter exposures and less motion blur. Both sensors are rolling shutter, so skew during fast moves stays.

**The lost LEDs matter less than before.** D-039 already gives LEDs their own mount. The Node-RED camera "light" toggle still writes to the white-LED pin, but nothing on the S401 is wired to it ([camera.cpp](https://github.com/Seeed-Studio/sscma-example-sg200x/blob/4926df702acba990b2ebd36a086f171a7964466e/solutions/sscma-node/main/node/camera.cpp); [DTS](https://github.com/Seeed-Studio/reCamera-OS/blob/0529ca0a8bc6b6b88a70c8b15e40d77c9e01cb82/external/build/boards/cv181x/sg2002_recamera_emmc/dts_riscv/sg2002_recamera_emmc.dts)) [inferred from source and schematics].

## What this changes in the 11-06 note

- **White LEDs: lost, not "may be lost".** The S401 has no white LEDs and no driver on that pin [schematics].
- **Mic: kept.** Both boards carry the mic and the speaker amp on the same connector pins. The red and blue status LEDs and the green power LED are kept too [schematics].
- **"Both do 1080p at 30 fps" hides a field difference.** The stock unit's 1080p is a 44° H crop, by our own calibration. The GC2053's 1080p is its full sensor, 84° H with the stock lens.
- **Software: no OS change on 0.2.1 or later** [source code]. Our scripts need new intrinsics, and `tagstream` needs distortion handling for a fisheye.
- **Install steps: still none from Seeed.** The likely procedure is above [inferred].
- **"160° horizontal" is the advertised figure.** Expect about 135° H on the GC2053 [calculated]. The 140° alternative is 131° H by Seeed's own sheet.
- **The 3 cm tag ≈ 70 px result fits a ~640 px-wide frame.** `start_tags.sh` ran detection at 640 × 480 with fx = 586 marked as an estimate. A full-field 640 px frame predicts about 73 px at 25 cm [calculated]. The 25 cm `z` came from those same pixels and that guessed fx, so the two numbers are not independent checks.
- **The fisheye pixel table holds.** This note's numbers (161 / 64 / 32 at 1920 px, 54 / 21 / 11 at 640 px) match within a few pixels. The 11-05 log adds that 20–30 px is hit or miss, so 640 px is too coarse for a 5 cm tag beyond about 30 cm.
- **Minimum object distance 0.2 m applies to the fisheye.** The stock S401 lens is set for 39–69.5 cm and can be refocused.
- **"Stock only in the China warehouse" (11-02).** The board's page now shows no stock status at all. Unconfirmed either way.

## Conclusion

The board is the easy part. The hard part is the pixel budget on the SG2002. Compared with the stock camera's 640 × 480 mode, a fisheye shrinks every centred tag by about 2.7–2.9× at the same width [calculated]. It only pays off if on-camera detection runs at 1280 px or wider at a usable rate, or the dock tags get bigger. That rate is the first number to measure after the swap, and nobody has published it.

Two findings change the framing. First, the stock 2002w at 1080p sees only 44° H, not 65°, so the stock S401 lens alone nearly doubles the view for $29.90. Second, on this sensor the $7.90 wide lens and the $9.90 fisheye land within a few degrees of each other on paper, while the wide lens keeps more pixels on the tag. Test both before choosing. Expect to own the image tuning. Seeed's public support for non-stock lenses has so far been tools and silence.
